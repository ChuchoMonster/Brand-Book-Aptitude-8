## Summary

- **Typography:** **Roobert** (paid display, Displaay Type Foundry) for hero, **Plus Jakarta Display/Sans** (free Google) for everything else. Free substitute for Roobert: **Bricolage Grotesque**.
- **Color discipline:** **Dark + signature yellow.** `#FFD131` is *the* identity signal — used exclusively for CTAs. Lavender-tinted body copy (`#D5D6E5`) is the brand's secret weapon for premium dark feel. Surface is `#14141C` with subtle purple-warm tint.
- **Voice signature:** Confident "Build it Better." tagline. Brand explicitly tells you what they DON'T do — Inbound Marketing, Agency Creative, Outsourced Delivery. That negation is the differentiator.
- **Conceptual signature:** "We optimize / architect / implement / migrate Your CRM Operations" — verb-cycler hero. CRM as the center of an architectural diagram, with Aptitude 8 as the architect.
